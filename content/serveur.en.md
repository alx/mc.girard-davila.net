## Worlds

- **NewWorld (hub)** — main world, spawn with 5 gates to the other worlds
- **bedwars** — 4 BedWars arenas (Red vs Blue, plus a 3-player 1v1v1)
- **oneblock** — one-block minigame with progression
- **parkour** — timed courses
- **creative / flatland** — two creative worlds (classic and superflat)

## In game

- `/lobby` — return to the hub from any world
- Hub gates lead to each world (BedWars, OneBlock, Parkour, Creative, Flat)
- Skins supported for offline accounts via SkinsRestorer

> ℹ️ The server is self-hosted at home and remains **private (LAN)** for now.
