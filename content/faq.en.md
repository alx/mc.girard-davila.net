### Is the server public?
Not yet — it is reachable on the local network only for now. A public address will come later.

### Do I need to pay for Minecraft?
No; the server is offline-mode, so an offline account (or Ely.by) is enough to play.

### How do I hear about LAN parties?
Sign up with your email via the form at the top of the home page.

### What data do you collect?
Only the email (and optionally gamertag/message) you enter in the sign-up form, solely to contact you about LAN parties. Unsubscribe on request.

### Can I use WorldEdit?
Yes, in the two **creative** worlds (`//wand`).
